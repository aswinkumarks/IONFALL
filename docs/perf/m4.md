# M4: performance against PLAN §3.10 (2026-10-21)

Release templates throughout. Phone: the "Android Test" preset exported with
`--export-release` (release engine, shader baker on; signed with the debug
key so it updates the installed test app). Launch arguments and PERF lines go
through the app's external files directory (`launch_args.txt`, `perf.log`),
since Android release builds can't use `run-as`.

PERF lines are one per minute: frame p50/p95/p99, hitches (frames over
50 ms), GPU time, main-thread section times, peak draw calls / primitives /
particles, texture memory, thermal status and the render scale in force.
`--perf-log` also logs every hitch with that frame's section times and
pipeline compiles.

## Laptop (Intel Xe TGL GT2, Vulkan Mobile, 1920×1080 fullscreen)

Spire, offline, 8 combatants (7 bots + autopilot), 21 minutes each.

| Preset | Minutes at 60.0 fps | Frame p95 / p99 (ms) | Tick p99 (ms) | Peaks: draws / primitives / particles | Texture mem |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium (0.6 scale) | 21 / 21 | 16.7 / 16.7 every minute | 1.9–2.2 | 219 / 173k / 228 | 354 MiB |
| High (1.0, MSAA 2×, sun shadows) | after step-downs | 30.3 / 40.6 at 0.9 → 16.7 / 16.7 at 0.6 | 2.2–2.6 | 226 / 230k / 328 | 406–440 MiB |

High at full resolution is too heavy for this iGPU; adaptive quality stepped
the render scale 1.0 → 0.9 → 0.8 → 0.7 → 0.6 over the run (p95 36.8, 30.2,
25.0, 22.2 ms at each step) and then held 60 fps. No dedicated-GPU PC was
available, so the "High, 144 fps" row of §3.10 is **not measured**.

Texture memory as reported (`RENDER_TEXTURE_MEM_USED`) includes render
targets (shadow atlas, MSAA and glow buffers). Asset textures alone are about
53 MiB after M4 capped every texture at 1024² and VRAM-compressed the UBC body
and Spire props textures (the UBC ones were uncompressed RGBA8, ~21 MiB each
at 2048²).

## Mi 11X

### 20-minute soaks, 2026-10-21 (build 806b8a9: effect pooling, glow kept out of adaptive step-down)

Phone on battery over wireless adb, starting at 32–39 °C; laptop dedicated
server on Spire; phone runs autopilot; adaptive quality on.

| Run | Minutes meeting p95 ≤ 16.7 | Frame p95 / p99 (ms) | GPU p95 (ms) | Peak draws | End temp |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Medium, 8 combatants | 0 / 21 | 17.5–20.6 / 25–28 for 14 min; 22–32 / 30–67 after (two stall bursts, then throttled p50 18.1) | 12.3–13.6 | 271 | 42 °C |
| Low, 16 combatants | 10 / 21 | 16.7 / 16.7–22 in the first (quieter) match; 21.7–23.6 / 28–31 in the second | 10.5–12.3 | 327 | 41 °C |

Neither run meets §3.10. The GPU stays inside its 12 ms budget at p50, so
the misses are main-thread time and stalls:

- **Kill feed:** building a row of controls cost ~15 ms per kill on the
  phone (3 ms on the laptop). Rows are now built once and reused
  (0.07 ms per kill on the laptop). The first row's glyphs (up to 0.8 s once)
  are now drawn during the loading cover.
- **Draw calls:** with 16 players fighting, effect nodes and nameplates (2
  draws each: text and outline) pushed draws to 280–327. Cosmetic effects
  (muzzle flashes, impacts, spawn/death/pickup bursts) are now capped at
  60 × effects density live nodes, and nameplates cull beyond 30 m.
  Tracers, rockets and explosions are never capped.
- **Pipeline compiles** still land in fights (`spec+1`, 60–150 ms) a few
  times per match despite the warm-up.
- **Heat:** after ~15 minutes on battery the phone slows (p50 18.1 ms with
  unchanged GPU time) while Android still reports thermal status 0, so
  adaptive quality only sees frame time, and render scale can't buy back
  main-thread time.
- Match-end results cost 60–140 ms once (profile save); acceptable behind
  the results screen.

Earlier short runs (before the soaks):

Client on Medium (0.6 scale, no FXAA, no effect lights), Spire, joined to a
laptop dedicated server with 8 combatants, autopilot:

| Run | Frame p95 / p99 (ms) | GPU p50 / p95 (ms) | Hitches/min | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Quiet minutes (no fire nearby) | 16.7 / 18–25 | 11.1–11.3 / 12.5–12.9 | 0–4 | meets §3.10 |
| In combat | 19–25 / 25–34 | 10.9–12.1 / 13.4–14.7 | 2–8 | misses p95 |
| 21-min soak (pre-pooling) | 16.7 / 18–25 for 10 min, then 19–25 / 25–34 at 0.5 scale | ~11 | 0–8 | adaptive dropped scale; didn't help |

What the measurements say:

- **GPU is not the limit.** GPU time sits at 11–12 ms (inside the 12 ms
  budget) and barely moves with render scale (0.4 vs 0.6: −1 ms) or with
  combat. The fixed cost is the lightmapped map itself.
- **Misses come from the main thread and from pipeline compiles.** Every
  hitch logged with `--perf-log` came with `pipelines[spec+N]`: specialization
  compiles on Adreno cost 60–150 ms each, triggered by new light/material
  combinations (first omni-lit object, glow toggled, first effect of a kind).
  Effect lights off and the lit warm-up removed most of them; the pooled
  effect nodes and glow-free step-down target the rest.
- Effects at full density add 2–3 ms of main-thread time in combat
  (`effects=1.0` vs `0.0`).
- The reflection probe costs ~0.9 ms GPU; FXAA ~0.5 ms. Both are off on
  Medium for the phone (probe kept on Medium elsewhere).
- Budgets held throughout: ≤ 258 draw calls (target ≤ 250 is marginal),
  ≤ 176k primitives, ≤ 250 particles, ≤ 185 MiB texture memory.
- Heat: runs while charging at 42 °C+ battery were consistently worse; the
  thermal status stayed 0 and `getThermalHeadroom` returns NaN on this phone,
  so adaptive quality only reacts to frame time here.

The render-thread experiment (`thread_model=2`) printed render-thread and
RID-leak errors on the laptop and was reverted before a phone measurement.
