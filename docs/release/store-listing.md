# Google Play store listing (draft)

**App name (30):** IONFALL: Arena Shooter

**Short description (80):** Fast sci-fi arena FPS. Play bots, LAN or online with friends. No ads.

**Full description:**

Drop into the Ion Trials: fast, skill-based arena combat aboard an orbital
research station.

• Fast movement: sprint, slide, double jump and mantle across two arenas,
  Meridian Station and Halcyon Spire.
• Six guns, from the Pulse Rifle and Scatter Gun to the Rail Sniper and
  Arc Launcher, plus frag grenades and melee. Power weapons spawn on the map.
• Free-for-all and Team Deathmatch.
• Play solo against bots with four difficulty levels.
• Play with friends on the same Wi-Fi, or over the internet with an invite
  code. Host a lobby, pick the map, mode and match length, and start when
  everyone's in.
• Cross-play between Android and PC (Windows, Linux).
• Touch controls designed for one-thumb play, with a layout editor, aim
  assist and auto-fire options. Full keyboard and mouse support on PC.
• Level up to unlock characters and colours. No ads, no in-app purchases.
• Accessibility: captions for important sounds, damage-direction indicators,
  team shapes as well as colours, reduced flashes and screen shake, HUD
  scaling and fully remappable controls.
