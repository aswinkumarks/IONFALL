# IONFALL 1.0.0

The first release: a fast sci-fi arena shooter for Android, Windows and
Linux.

## Play

- **Single player** against bots (four difficulties) on Meridian Station or
  Halcyon Spire, free-for-all or Team Deathmatch, 5–20 minute matches.
- **Multiplayer:** host a lobby on your local network or over the internet,
  pick map, mode and length, and start when everyone's in; after each match
  everyone returns to the lobby. Join from the game list (LAN and online
  games) or with an IP address or 6-letter invite code. Android, Windows and
  Linux play together.
- **Dedicated server** for LAN (Linux and Windows), up to 16 players.

## Game

- Movement: sprint, slide, double jump, mantle.
- Six guns, frag grenades, melee and power weapons on the map.
- Kill feed, death cam, results with XP, levels and unlockable characters
  and colours.

## Controls and accessibility

- Touch: one-thumb cluster around Fire, floating move stick, layout editor,
  aim assist, auto-fire.
- Keyboard and mouse: fully remappable; hold or toggle for crouch, sprint
  and aim.
- Captions, damage and gunfire indicators, team shapes as well as colours,
  reduce flashes and screen shake, HUD scaling, separate sensitivities,
  invert Y, FOV.

## Known limits

- Internet play needs Epic Online Services (free; anonymous sign-in, no
  account). Not available in the web build.
- Tested on a Xiaomi Mi 11X and an Intel Iris Xe laptop; other devices may
  perform differently. Phone hosts are capped at 4 players.
