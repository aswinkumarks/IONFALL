# IONFALL privacy policy

*Effective date: [publication date]. Publisher: [publisher name / contact
email].*

IONFALL is an arena shooter you can play alone, on your local network, or
over the internet with friends. We don't run accounts, ads or analytics,
and we don't sell or share data for advertising.

## Data on your device

Your profile (player name, level, XP, match stats, unlocks) and settings
are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

## Playing with others

When you join or host a match, the other players in it receive your player
name, your character and colour choices, a random per-install identifier
used to recognise you if you reconnect, and your in-game actions. On a
local network, hosts announce their game name, map and mode to devices on
that network. Players you connect to directly can see your IP address, as
with any peer-to-peer game.

## Internet play (Epic Online Services)

Internet play uses Epic Online Services (EOS), provided by Epic Games, Inc.
When you first host or browse internet games, the game signs in to EOS
anonymously with a device credential created on your device. EOS receives:

- an anonymous device credential and the EOS user ID it maps to,
- your device model (for example "Android M2012K11AI"),
- your player name, used as your display name,
- for games you host: the invite code, game name, map, mode and player
  count, which other IONFALL players can see in the online games list,
- your IP address and game traffic when it is relayed through Epic's
  servers.

Epic processes this data to provide sign-in, lobbies and connections. See
Epic's privacy policy: https://www.epicgames.com/site/privacypolicy. Data is
encrypted in transit. Internet play is optional; single player and local
network play don't contact Epic.

To ask for deletion of data associated with your EOS user, contact us at
[contact email] and we will forward the request to Epic, or contact Epic
directly through its privacy request process.

## Children

IONFALL is not directed at children under 13.

We'll update this page if the game's data handling changes, and change the
